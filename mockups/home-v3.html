The world is getting better. The world is getting worse. Both are true.

Not a verdict, but an instrument. Every question is read at each magnification — from the ten-thousand-year arc to a single lived day — because the answer changes depending on how closely you look.

Hope

What is quietly, measurably improving?

Fewer children die, lives run longer, extreme poverty keeps falling.

Despair

What is worsening, fragile, or still intolerable?

Displacement, emissions, and great-power risk are all climbing again.

Confusion

What is uncertain, contradictory, or hidden by the average?

A global mean can hold catastrophe and calm inside the very same line.
